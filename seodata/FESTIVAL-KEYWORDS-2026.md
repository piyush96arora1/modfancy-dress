# Festival Keyword Research: modfancydress.com (Sep 2026 → Feb 2027)

Prepared 2026-09-23. No repo files were modified.

## 0. How to read this

**Sources**
- **Semrush (June 2026 snapshot, India DB)**: `seodata/` broad-match, three gap.keywords exports, organic Positions, Competitors. Volumes are national monthly averages. The gap exports only hold keywords that competitors rank for, so seasonal adult terms (Halloween head terms, Santa suits) are under-represented. A missing Semrush figure does **not** mean no demand.
- **Google autocomplete** (`suggestqueries.google.com`, `hl=en&gl=in`, about 230 seeds, pulled 2026-09-23). A suggestion shows that people really type the query, but it carries no volume. Rows marked **AC** are autocomplete-confirmed only. Raw output is in `ac1.json` to `ac6.json` in this folder.
- **Google Trends was blocked**: HTTP 429 on both the `/api/explore` endpoint and the explore page, from curl and from WebFetch. Seasonality below comes from the 12-month trend arrays Semrush returned for the keywords the site ranks for. For example, `ravan dress` peaks in Oct at 100, then Nov 79, then about 10 the rest of the year. Dance terms (kalbelia, kashmiri, semi-classical, bhangra) peak in Dec–Feb. Rent terms (`fancy dress costumes on rent near me`) peak in Oct, with a second rise in Jan.
- **Site inventory**: checked against the live `sitemap.xml` on 2026-09-23 (324 product URLs, 50 categories, 21 blog posts).

**Legend**
- **Intent:** C = commercial/transactional (someone looking to buy or rent), I = informational (ideas, how-to). "C/I" means mixed.
- **Page type:** CAT = category, PROD = product, BLOG = blog post, LOC = location page (`/fancy-dress-delhi`, `-noida`, `-gurgaon`), WS = wholesale pages.
- **Publish-by:** the date by which a page should be live or refreshed, 4–6 weeks before the search peak. **LATE** means we are already inside that 4–6 week window. For those, refresh existing pages that are already indexed and lean on GBP posts, rather than expecting a new URL to rank this season.
