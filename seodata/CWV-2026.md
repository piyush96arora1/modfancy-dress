## What the numbers say

- **Bytes are fixed.** Image weight fell 85–95% on every page. Four of the five pages are
  now under the 1.5MB target; `/products` is at 1.57MB.
- **LCP is still high, but images no longer cause it.** On the after runs the LCP element
  is text on category pages and on home (a `<p>` intro, a heading `<span>`), with 0ms load
  delay and 11–18s of *render delay*. That is main-thread/JS time, not bytes, which is
  Task 8's scope. On /products and Gabbar the LCP element is the product image, and there
  too most of the time is render delay (6.7s and 5.4s), not the image load (1.8s to 3.6s).
  The run-to-run spread (dandiya 6.2s before, 11.8s after, with 85% fewer bytes) is that
  render-delay noise.
- **What is left on `/`:** `/api/products` prefetch (1.77MB JSON, from `AssetPreloader`, for
  Task 8); the seasonal hero banner, whose desktop source is a 474KB original product photo
  (`rainbow-panel-mirror-work-garba-chaniya-choli.webp`), fetched even on mobile; and the
  304KB mobile banner.

## Cache headers (Task 7 step 1)

`cache-control: no-cache` was a measuring mistake. Supabase Storage answers **HEAD**
(`curl -I`) with `no-cache` on every object. **GET** returns the object's own cacheControl:

| Object | GET cache-control |
|---|---|
| uploaded with `cacheControl: '31536000'` (admin uploader, all variants) | `public, max-age=31536000` |
| uploaded without cacheControl (older script imports) | `public, max-age=3600` (storage default) |

No project setting needs changing. Every variant is written with a one-year cache.
`scripts/import-supplier-catalog.ts` and `scripts/import-collection.ts` now pass
`cacheControl` too. Originals that were uploaded without it keep 3600s: they were not
re-uploaded, because the originals are not to be touched.

## Variant backfill

`scripts/generate-image-variants.ts --apply` (then `--verify`):
- 651 originals, 1,953 variants written, over three runs: a 3-image trial, the main run of
  632 originals, then 16 category-only images.
- On the main run, original bytes were 91.7MB. Variants: w400 10.0MB (16KB average), w800
  25.1MB (41KB), w1600 38.2MB (62KB).
- `--verify`: 1,953 of 1,956 variant URLs return 200 `image/webp`.
- The 3 that fail belong to one original, `products-webp/kathak-dance-dress-anarkali-style-1.webp`.
  That original itself returns 400 (the object is missing), so it is broken on the live
  site too.
