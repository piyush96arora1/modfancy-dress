Ramleela 2026 runs from **11 to 20 October**, ending with Dussehra (Vijayadashami) on **Tuesday 20 October**. School Dussehra programmes and fancy dress rounds usually fall in the days before. Whether you are dressing one child as Hanuman or a committee's whole cast for a ten-night run, here is what each main character needs, what to look for in the costume, and how renting works in Delhi.

## Ram: crowned king or forest exile?

Decide which Ram the script needs first, because the two look nothing alike.

- **Raja Ram** is the crowned king of Ayodhya, for the coronation scene or leading the Dussehra procession. Our [Raja Ram dress](/products/raja-ram-fancy-dress) is a red kurta with a gold zari yoke, mirror work and a fringed hem.
- **Vanvasi Ram** is the Ram of the fourteen years in the forest, dressed plainly in saffron. The [Vanvasi Ram fancy dress](/products/vanvasi-ram-fancy-dress) comes with a bow and a quiver, so the child has something to hold through a speaking scene.

For Dashrath, Janak or any other king in the court, the [Hindu Raja costume](/products/hindu-raja-fancy-dress-costume-for-boys) with dhoti and crown covers it. There is no separate Lakshman costume in our range. A second Vanvasi set works, ideally with a different coloured garland so the audience can tell the brothers apart.

## Sita: Ayodhya queen or vanvas saree

The same rule applies. For the palace and swayamvar scenes, the [Rani Sita costume](/products/rani-sita-fancy-dress-costume-for-ramleela) is in gold and jewel tones. For the forest, Panchavati and Ashok Vatika scenes, the [vanvasi Sita saree](/products/vanvasi-sita-saree-dress) is a plain saffron saree with a cream and gold border, for girls aged 3 to 9. It also doubles as a Meera Bai costume.

Hanuman is one of the most popular Ramleela parts, and a school play often has several Hanumans on stage. Choose by how big the part is:

- **Lead role:** the [red velvet Hanuman costume](/products/hanuman-red-fancy-dress) has gold zari stripes and a jewelled yoke that catches hall lighting.
- **Standing apart from the other Hanumans:** the [yellow Hanuman dress](/products/hanuman-yellow-fancy-dress) in mustard gold looks different next to a row of red ones.

Every Hanuman needs a gada. Our [foam Hanuman gada](/products/hanuman-gada-for-drama-fancy-dress) looks heavy on stage but is safe for children to swing.

## Ravan: the costume, the mukut and the mask

Ravan is the part older children enjoy most. The [Ravan fancy dress](/products/ravan-fancy-dress) is black with gold booti print, a jewelled yoke and gold fringe. Black and gold is the classic Lankesh palette and reads as the villain from the back row.

The ten-head **Ravan mukut** makes the look. We don't list a mukut on the website, so WhatsApp us to ask what we have in the shop before your event. If you are making one at home, a ten-head crown cut from card and painted gold works on stage, provided it is light. A heavy mukut slips, and a child can't deliver lines while holding it up. Keep a mask or mukhota short enough for the child to see their feet, and check that they can speak clearly through it.
