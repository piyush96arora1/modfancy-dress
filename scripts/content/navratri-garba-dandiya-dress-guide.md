## Garba dress vs dandiya dress: is there a difference?

People use the two terms together, and in practice the same outfit works for both. A garba dress for women is the chaniya choli: a flared, embroidered skirt (chaniya), a fitted blouse (choli) and a dupatta. Garba is a circular dance, so the flare matters most. Dandiya raas is danced in pairs with sticks, so some people prefer sleeves that don't get in the way and a skirt that doesn't catch a partner's feet. The mirror work, bandhani prints and bright colours are the same. Browse both on our [dandiya dress](/category/dandiya-dress) and [garba dress](/category/garba-dress) pages.

## Garba dress for men and boys (kediyu)

The men's garba outfit is the **kediyu** (also spelt kedia or kediya): a short top gathered at the chest that flares into frills at the waist. It is worn with a dhoti or churidar and usually a pagdi (turban). The flare does for men what the chaniya does for women and opens up on every spin.

## Couple dandiya outfits

Matching exactly usually looks like a uniform. What works better:

- Choose her chaniya choli first, because it has the most colour.
- Pick one colour from its border, such as the maroon or the mirror-work green, and use it for his kediyu, pagdi or dupatta.
- Keep the metal the same: gold zari with gold, silver mirror work with silver.

## Garba dress for kids and school Navratri days

Many of our October enquiries are for school: a Navratri day, a garba competition, or a "traditional dress of Gujarat" round in a fancy dress competition. A good garba costume for a child has three parts: a lehenga that flares when they spin, a choli with mirror work or embroidery, and a dupatta. The mirror work (shisha embroidery) is what makes a group of children sparkle under hall lights.

What to avoid:

- **Heavy, many-layered lehengas.** They look good in photos but tire a child out before the performance ends.
- **Stone work on the hem.** Stones come loose during fast garba footwork.
- **Big sizes to "grow into".** A skirt that is too long gets stepped on in the first round.

For children under six, a simple half-lehenga with a comfortable blouse is plenty. Freedom to move matters more than detail.

Ready options for girls include the [traditional Navratri chaniya choli](/products/traditional-navratri-chaniya-choli-fancy-dress), the [Gujarati garba dance costume](/products/gujarati-garba-dance-fancy-dress-costume) and the [blue garba dance costume](/products/blue-polyester-garba-dance-costume-fancy-dress) (5–7 years). Boys can wear the kediya sets above.

**Dandiya sticks.** Get sticks the right length for your child's height. Sticks that are too long clash with the next child, and sticks that are too short make the choreography look wrong.

**For a competition,** add a small bindi, bangles and a dupatta pinned at the shoulder. Practise a one-line introduction such as "I am wearing the traditional dress of Gujarat, worn for garba during Navratri."
